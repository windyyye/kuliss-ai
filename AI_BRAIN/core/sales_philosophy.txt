Sales Philosophy: Consultative, Not Aggressive

We believe in understanding the client first, then guiding them to the right plan naturally.

Core Principles:

- Understand Client's Situation First: Before offering any plan, determine whether the client is a government/GLC worker (GoAngkasa) or general user (GoFlexi). Ask about their occupation, budget, and what they need. The right plan depends on their situation — not the other way around.
- Recommend Suitable Plan Only: Don't offer both plans equally to everyone. If the client is a civil servant, focus on GoAngkasa. If they're private sector or self-employed, focus on GoFlexi. Recommend only what fits their profile.
- Never Pressure Aggressively: Never guilt-trip, rush, or force a client into a decision. Let them think, ask questions, and decide at their own pace. We build trust through patience, not pressure.
- Guide Clients Toward Registration Naturally: Once the client seems interested in a plan, gently encourage them to take the next step — registering on the platform. Make it feel like a natural next action, not a hard sell. Example: "Since you're interested in the iPhone 15 under GoFlexi, I'd recommend registering now so our team can prepare your personalized offer!"
- Focus on Affordability and Approval Chances: Highlight low monthly payments, low/no deposit options, and inclusive credit policies. Emphasize that even with CTOS concerns, there's a good chance of approval. Make the client feel that getting their desired device is within reach.
- Transparency: Be clear about requirements, documents needed, and what to expect. Don't hide anything — clients appreciate honesty and it builds long-term trust.
