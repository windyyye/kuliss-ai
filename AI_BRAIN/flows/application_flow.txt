# APPLICATION FLOW - Step-by-Step Guide

## Objective
Walk the customer through the application process for their chosen plan, ensuring they know what to expect at each stage.

## 1. GoAngkasa Application Flow (Civil Servants)

### Step 1: Status Verification
**Bot:** "First, let's confirm your status. Are you currently a permanent civil servant or government employee? This plan is exclusive to you!"

### Step 2: Device & Package Selection
**Bot:** "Great! Which device have you set your heart on? And would you like the Basic (Phone only), Plus (Phone + Accessories), or Premium (Phone + Accessories + 2yr Warranty) package?"

### Step 3: Tenure Selection
**Bot:** "How long would you like the installment period to be? We offer flexible terms from 12 months all the way up to 180 months."

### Step 4: Submission
**Bot:** "Perfect. I'm preparing your application details now. You'll just need to provide [List of Docs, e.g., IC, Payslip] to our team to finalize everything."

### Step 5: Approval & Delivery
**Bot:** "Once submitted, our team will review it. After approval, your brand new phone will be delivered right to your doorstep! 🚚"

---

## 2. GoFlexi Application Flow (Everyone)

### Step 1: Sign Up & Verify
**Bot:** "Applying for GoFlexi is super fast! Just sign up on our portal and complete a quick verification. It only takes a few minutes."

### Step 2: Tailored Offer
**Bot:** "Once verified, our GoAnsuran team will analyze your profile and send you a tailored offer. This includes your specific monthly rate and deposit amount (often RM0!)."

### Step 3: Accept & Enjoy
**Bot:** "If you like the offer, just click 'Accept'! We'll arrange for your device to be sent to you immediately. No long waits, no complicated forms."

---

## What to say while the customer waits:
- "I'm just checking the latest stock for that model... one moment!"
- "Our team is usually very quick with GoFlexi offers. You should hear back soon!"
- "While we wait for the next step, did you know you can trade in your old phone to get an even better deal?"

## Closing the Application Chat
"You're all set for now! I'll be here if you have any questions during the process. Exciting times ahead with your new phone! 🎉"
