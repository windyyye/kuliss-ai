# PLAN COMPARISON FLOW - GoAngkasa vs GoFlexi

## Objective
Clearly explain the differences between the two main plans to help the customer choose the right one.

## Comparison Overview

| Feature | GoAngkasa | GoFlexi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Audience** | Civil Servants / Govt Employees | Everyone (Public/Private Sector) |
| **Payment Type** | Installment (Ownership) | Flexible Subscription |
| **Tenure** | Up to 180 months | Monthly (Flexible) |
| **Deposit** | Structured | Low to RM0 Deposit |
| **Upgrade Policy** | Trade-in available | Upgrade anytime |
| **Packages** | 3 Tiers (Basic to Premium) | Tailored Offers |

## Detailed Plan Breakdown

### GoAngkasa: The Long-Term Choice
"GoAngkasa is our flagship plan for civil servants. It's designed for stability and ownership."
- **3 Package Tiers:**
  1. **Phone Only:** Just the device.
  2. **Phone + Accessories:** Device plus essential gear.
  3. **Phone + Accessories + 2yr Warranty:** Full peace of mind.
- **Key Benefit:** Very long tenure (up to 180 months) makes monthly payments extremely low.

### GoFlexi: The Flexible Choice
"GoFlexi is for everyone who wants the latest tech without the long-term commitment."
- **How it works:** Sign up, get verified, and receive a tailored offer based on your profile.
- **Key Benefit:** Low or RM0 deposit, and the freedom to upgrade to a newer model whenever you're ready.

## When to Recommend Which?

### Recommend GoAngkasa only if:
- The customer is a civil servant.

### Recommend GoFlexi if:
- The customer is from the private sector or self-employed.
- They like changing phones every 1-2 years.
- They want a fast, flexible process with minimal upfront cost.
- They prefer a subscription-style model over traditional debt.

## Conversational Tip
"Think of GoAngkasa like buying a house with a long-term loan, and GoFlexi like a premium car subscription where you can always drive the latest model!"
