GoAnsuran Company Information

Company Name: GoAnsuran
Tagline: "Tech You Love, Made Affordable" / "Simply Affordable"
Website: https://goansuran-user-uat.web.app/

Overview:
GoAnsuran is a Malaysian technology platform that makes premium gadgets accessible to everyone through flexible monthly payment plans. Instead of requiring large upfront payments that put the latest smartphones out of reach for many Malaysians, GoAnsuran offers structured installment and subscription options that spread costs over time. The company operates at the intersection of technology retail and financial accessibility, serving both the general public and specific segments such as government employees.

Mission:
GoAnsuran's mission is to make modern technology accessible to all Malaysians. The company believes that everyone deserves to enjoy the benefits of premium devices, whether for work, education, entertainment, or staying connected with loved ones. GoAnsuran provides the freedom to upgrade, switch, or continue using devices based on evolving needs, ensuring customers are never locked into outdated technology.

Core Value Propositions:

1. Flexible Payments
GoAnsuran offers payment plans tailored to individual needs and financial situations. Whether a customer prefers a shorter commitment with higher monthly payments or a longer tenure with more affordable installments, there is an option available. The platform serves diverse income levels and financial profiles.

2. Upgrade Anytime
Technology evolves rapidly, and GoAnsuran ensures customers are not left behind. Subscribers can trade in their current device and switch to a newer model at any time. This upgrade freedom is central to the GoFlexi subscription model and distinguishes GoAnsuran from traditional device financing.

3. Wide Selection
GoAnsuran carries popular devices from the world's most trusted brands. The catalog includes products from Apple, Samsung, Google, ASUS, HONOR, Infinix, iQOO, Nothing, OnePlus, OPPO, Realme, Red Magic, TECNO, Vivo, and Xiaomi. This covers the full spectrum from premium flagships to budget-friendly performers, ensuring every customer finds a device that suits their needs and budget.

4. Simple Process
GoAnsuran has eliminated the complex paperwork and lengthy approval processes common in traditional financing. The online application is fast, user-friendly, and designed for the mobile-first Malaysian consumer. Verification is quick, and approval notifications are sent promptly.

Two Main Offerings:
GoAnsuran operates through two distinct plan types, each designed for a specific customer segment:

- GoAngkasa: A specialized device installment plan designed exclusively for Malaysian civil servants and government employees. It offers structured installment periods ranging from 12 to 180 months with three package tiers (Phone Only, Phone + Accessories, Phone + Accessories + 2 Years Warranty). GoAngkasa is known for its inclusive credit policy, considering applicants with higher DSR/CTOS/CCRIS profiles.

- GoFlexi: A flexible subscription plan open to the general public. It features low or zero deposits, fixed monthly subscription fees, and the freedom to upgrade devices at any time. The process follows three simple steps: Sign Up and Verify, receive a Tailored Offer from the GoAnsuran team, and Accept and Enjoy the new device.

Market Context:
GoAnsuran operates in the Malaysian market with all prices quoted in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). The platform serves a bilingual audience, communicating in both English and Bahasa Melayu to ensure accessibility across all demographics. GoAnsuran's approach is particularly well-suited to the Malaysian market, where many consumers seek flexible payment options for premium technology.
