GoAnsuran Eligibility Criteria

This document provides detailed eligibility criteria for both GoAngkasa and GoFlexi plans. Use this information to guide customers who ask about qualifying for either program.

========================================
GOANGKASA ELIGIBILITY
========================================

Who Can Apply:
GoAngkasa is designed exclusively for government or GLC (Government-Linked Company) workers. The following groups are eligible:

1. Malaysian Civil Servants (Kakitangan Kerajaan Malaysia)
   - Federal government employees across all ministries and departments
   - State government employees
   - Local government (PBT - Pihak Berkuasa Tempatan) employees

2. Government-Linked Company (GLC) Employees
   - Employees of companies with significant government ownership or affiliation
   - This includes statutory bodies and government agencies

3. Cooperative Employees with Active ASBN
   - Employees who are members of cooperatives registered with Angkatan Simpanan Nasional Berhad (ASBN)
   - Must have an active ASBN membership in good standing

Required Documents:
- Current month's payslip
- Previous month's payslip
- CTOS report
- Latest EPF statement (optional)

Key Requirements:
- Must be a Malaysian citizen or permanent resident
- Must hold a valid government staff ID or equivalent official identification
- Must have an active ASBN (Angkatan Simpanan Nasional Berhad) account

Credit Profile Considerations:
- Bad CTOS can still apply — GoAngkasa evaluates applications holistically
- Applicants with higher DSR (Debt Service Ratio) are welcome to apply
- The primary qualification is verified civil servant or government employee status
- Credit assessment is not purely score-based

Salary Deduction:
- Monthly installments are deducted directly from salary via Angkasa's programme
- This ensures automatic, hassle-free payments with no risk of late fees

Approval Timeline:
- Approval typically takes 2-3 days

Important Note for Customers:
If a customer asks about eligibility and they are NOT a civil servant or government employee, politely redirect them to the GoFlexi plan, which is open to everyone.

========================================
GOFLEXI ELIGIBILITY
========================================

Who Can Apply:
GoFlexi is open to the general public with no specific employment requirements. The following groups are eligible, each with their own document requirements:

1. Private Sector Workers
   Required documents:
   - Current month's payslip
   - Previous month's payslip
   - CTOS report
   - Latest EPF statement (optional)

2. Self-Employed
   Required documents:
   - SSM Business Registration (SSM corporate profile)
   - Current month's bank statement
   - Previous month's bank statement
   - Bank statement from 2 months ago
   - CTOS report

3. Platform Riders / Drivers (Lalamove, Grab, FoodPanda & etc)
   Required documents:
   - Current month's income statement
   - Previous month's income statement
   - Income statement from 2 months ago
   - CTOS report

4. Commissioned Agent (MLM, Cuckoo, Insurance, Property & etc)
   Required documents:
   - Current month's commission statement
   - Previous month's commission statement
   - Commission statement from 2 months ago
   - CTOS report

5. Students (minimum 18 years old)
   Required documents:
   - Student ID picture (front and back)
   - Current month's bank statement
   - Previous month's bank statement
   - Bank statement from 2 months ago

6. Others (Foreign Workers, Housewives, Retirees, Pensioners & etc)
   Required documents:
   - Current month's bank statement
   - Previous month's bank statement
   - Bank statement from 2 months ago
   - CTOS report

Key Points:
- No guarantor required for any category.
- Bad CTOS can still apply — GoFlexi is suitable for clients with low or no CTOS score.
- Deposit is required — amount depends on device and applicant profile.
- Approval takes 2-3 working days.
- All applicants must complete the e-KYC process (personal data, identity verification, residency address, emergency contact, occupation, phone model selection, email verification).

========================================
GUIDANCE FOR CHATBOT CONVERSATIONS
========================================

When the occupation of a customer is collected or client asks "Am I eligible?" or "Who can apply?":

1. First, determine their employment type:
   - Ask: "Are you a government employee or civil servant?" or "Kakitangan kerajaan ke?"
   
2. If YES (civil servant/government employee):
   - Recommend GoAngkasa as the primary option (better long-term value)
   - Mention GoFlexi as an alternative if they prefer shorter commitments
   - Highlight the inclusive credit policy for GoAngkasa

3. If NO (private sector/self-employed/student):
   - Recommend GoFlexi as their option
   - Highlight the RM 0 deposit and upgrade freedom
   - Guide them to goansuran.com.my to sign up

4. If unsure about their status:
   - Explain both plans briefly and ask which one interests them
   - Offer to help them determine the best fit based on their situation
