GoFlexi Plan Overview

What is GoFlexi?
GoFlexi is an installment plan for general users — open to everyone, not just civil servants. Customers pay monthly rental/installment payments on a rent-to-own or flexible ownership basis. GoFlexi is especially suitable for clients with low or no CTOS score, making premium devices accessible to a wider audience.

Key Features:
- Monthly rental / installment payments — predictable fixed monthly amounts.
- Rent to own / flexible ownership — choose to own the device eventually or stay flexible.
- Suitable for clients with low or no CTOS score — bad credit is not a dealbreaker.
- Can upgrade, return, or continue depending on the package — full flexibility at every stage.
- Mobile Device Management (MDM) is applied to all mobiles offered under GoFlexi for security and device management purposes.

Target Audience:
GoFlexi is open to the general public with no specific employment requirement. This includes private sector employees, self-employed individuals, platform riders/drivers (Grab, Lalamove, FoodPanda, etc.), commissioned agents (MLM, Cuckoo, Insurance, Property, etc.), students (minimum 18 years old), foreign workers, housewives, retirees, pensioners, and more. This broad inclusivity is a key differentiator from GoAngkasa, which is limited to civil servants.

How GoFlexi Works — 8-Step e-KYC Application Process:

Step 1: Personal Data
The applicant provides their basic personal information — full name as per IC, date of birth, IC number, and other required personal details.

Step 2: Identity Verification
The applicant undergoes identity verification, typically by uploading a photo of their MyKad (IC) and a live selfie for facial matching. This is a standard e-KYC security measure.

Step 3: Residency Address
The applicant provides their current residential address for delivery and verification purposes.

Step 4: Emergency Contact
The applicant provides an emergency contact person — name, relationship, and phone number.

Step 5: Occupation
The applicant provides their employment details. This step determines which supporting documents are required to proceed with the application.

Step 6: Phone Model
The applicant selects their preferred phone model from the available GoFlexi catalog.

Step 7: Email Verification
The applicant verifies their email address by clicking a confirmation link sent to their inbox.

Step 8: Application Review & Phone Offer
The GoAnsuran team reviews the application and creates a personalized phone offer. A team member reaches out to present the offer including deposit amount and monthly subscription fee. Approval typically takes 2-3 working days.

Pricing Structure:
GoFlexi uses a simple two-component pricing model:
- Deposit: An upfront payment to start the subscription. Deposit is required — the amount depends on the device and the applicant's profile.
- Monthly Subscription Fee: A fixed monthly rental/installment amount that varies by device model and storage variant.

There is no complex tenure matrix to navigate. Each device variant has a clear deposit amount and monthly fee, making it easy to understand exactly what will be paid.

Available Brands:
GoFlexi currently features devices from five major brands:
- Apple: Various iPhone models from the latest releases to reliable previous generations
- Samsung: Galaxy S series, Galaxy A series, and Galaxy Z Fold/Flip foldable devices
- HONOR: Magic series and X series with competitive features
- OPPO: Find series and Reno series for photography enthusiasts
- Google: Pixel series with pure Android experience and leading camera technology

Brand and model availability may change as new devices are released and inventory is updated.

Upgrade, Return, or Continue:
GoFlexi's core value proposition is flexibility. Depending on the package, customers can:
- Upgrade: Trade in their current subscribed device and switch to a newer model.
- Return: Return the device if it no longer suits their needs.
- Continue: Keep using the device on an ongoing basis.

This ensures subscribers are never locked into a rigid arrangement and always have options.

Mobile Device Management (MDM):
All mobiles offered under GoFlexi come with MDM (Mobile Device Management) applied. This is a standard security and device management measure that protects both the customer and GoAnsuran.

Key Differentiators from GoAngkasa:
- Open to everyone (no civil servant requirement)
- Suitable for low or no CTOS score applicants
- Simple deposit + monthly fee structure (no complex tenure matrix)
- Upgrade, return, or continue flexibility
- MDM applied to all devices
- Personalized offers from a human team
- Approval in 2-3 working days
