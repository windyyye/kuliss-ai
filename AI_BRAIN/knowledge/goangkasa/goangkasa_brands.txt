GoAngkasa Brands Overview

GoAngkasa offers a comprehensive selection of mobile devices from 15 world-leading brands, catering to every segment of the Malaysian civil servant market. From premium flagship devices to budget-friendly performance phones, our catalog ensures that every government employee can find a device that fits their needs and financial plan.

1. Apple: The pinnacle of premium smartphones. Our catalog includes the latest iPhone series, known for their exceptional build quality, powerful A-series chips, and the seamless iOS ecosystem. These are the preferred choice for users seeking a long-lasting, high-status flagship experience.

2. Samsung: Offering the most diverse range, Samsung provides the Galaxy S series for flagship seekers, the Galaxy Z series for those wanting innovative foldable technology, and the Galaxy A and M series for reliable mid-range and budget performance.

3. Google: The Pixel series offers the "pure" Android experience as intended by Google. These devices are renowned for their industry-leading computational photography and immediate software updates, making them a favorite for tech enthusiasts.

4. ASUS: Specializing in high-performance hardware, ASUS brings the ROG Phone series for the ultimate gaming experience and the Zenfone series for those who prefer powerful yet compact flagship devices.

5. HONOR: Formerly a sub-brand of Huawei, HONOR has established itself with the Magic series (flagship) and X series (mid-range), offering impressive display technology and battery life at competitive prices.

6. Infinix: A rapidly growing brand focusing on the budget-friendly segment. The Note, Zero, and Hot series provide large screens and fast charging capabilities, making them excellent value-for-money options for everyday use.

7. iQOO: A performance-focused brand that bridges the gap between mainstream and gaming phones. iQOO devices are known for their high refresh rate displays and top-tier processing power, aimed at younger, tech-savvy users.

8. Nothing: Bringing a unique aesthetic to the market, Nothing's Phone series features the iconic Glyph Interface and a clean, bloatware-free Android experience (Nothing OS), appealing to those who value design and simplicity.

9. OnePlus: Known as the original "flagship killer," OnePlus continues to offer high-end specifications and the smooth OxygenOS experience, providing a premium feel at a slightly more accessible price point than traditional flagships.

10. OPPO: A leader in mobile photography and design. The Find series represents their high-end innovation, while the Reno series focuses on portrait photography and sleek aesthetics, making them popular among social media enthusiasts.

11. Realme: Focused on providing "leap-forward" technology to the youth. The Number and GT series offer high-end features like ultra-fast charging and powerful chipsets at prices that redefine value for money.

12. Red Magic: The dedicated gaming arm of Nubia. These phones are built with active cooling fans, shoulder triggers, and extreme performance tuning, specifically designed for competitive mobile gamers.

13. TECNO: Another strong contender in the budget and mid-range segments. The Camon series focuses on camera innovation, while the Spark series provides reliable performance for entry-level users.

14. Vivo: Combining elegant design with advanced camera technology. The X series offers professional-grade photography features (often in partnership with ZEISS), while the V series is famous for its selfie capabilities and slim profiles.

15. Xiaomi: A global powerhouse offering everything from the premium Xiaomi 14 and 13 series to the highly popular Redmi series. Xiaomi is synonymous with high-spec hardware at disruptive prices across all market tiers.
