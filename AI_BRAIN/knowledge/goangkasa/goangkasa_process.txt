GoAngkasa Application Process

The GoAngkasa application process is streamlined to provide Malaysian civil servants and government employees with a hassle-free way to acquire the latest mobile technology. Our system is integrated with civil service verification protocols to ensure a secure and efficient experience from browsing to delivery.

Step 1: Eligibility Verification
The process begins with the verification of the applicant's status as a civil servant or government employee. This is a crucial step as the special installment rates and extended tenures are exclusively reserved for this group. Applicants may need to provide their government staff ID or other official credentials to proceed.

Step 2: Product Selection
Once verified, users can browse the extensive GoAngkasa catalog. The platform allows filtering by brand (e.g., Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi) or searching for specific models. Each product page provides detailed specifications and high-quality imagery to help users make the right choice.

Step 3: Package Customization
After selecting a model, the user chooses their preferred package tier:
- Phone Only: For the device alone.
- Phone + Accessories: Includes a case, screen protector, charger, and power bank.
- Phone + Accessories + 2 Years Warranty: The full bundle with extended protection.

Step 4: Tenure Selection
Users then select their desired installment tenure, ranging from 12 months up to 180 months. The system dynamically updates the monthly payment amount based on the selected package and tenure, allowing users to find the perfect balance between the total cost and monthly affordability.

Step 5: Application Submission
The user reviews their selection and submits the formal application. At this stage, additional documentation such as an ASBN (Angkasa) statement or recent payslips may be required to facilitate the credit assessment process.

Step 6: Approval and Credit Assessment
GoAngkasa is known for its inclusive credit policy. We consider applicants with higher DSR (Debt Service Ratio) or those who might have challenges with CTOS/CCRIS profiles, provided they meet the core civil servant criteria. The approval process is typically fast, with notifications sent via the platform or email.

Step 7: Device Delivery
Upon successful approval and finalization of the agreement, the device is prepared for dispatch. GoAngkasa ensures secure and tracked delivery to the applicant's specified address, whether it's their home or government office.

Key Advantages:
- No upfront payment required in many cases.
- Direct salary deduction options for easy management.
- Exclusive access to long-term tenures not available in the retail market.
- Comprehensive protection plans included in the bundle options.

By following these steps, government employees can upgrade their mobile devices with confidence and financial ease.
