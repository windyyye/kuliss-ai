GoFlexi Pricing Guide

GoFlexi is designed with simplicity and transparency in mind, offering a straightforward pricing structure that makes premium gadgets accessible without the complexity often found in traditional financing or other subscription models.

Simple Pricing Structure:
Unlike the complex matrices used in other plans like GoAngkasa, GoFlexi pricing consists of only two primary components:
1. Deposit: The upfront payment required to start your subscription. This can be as low as RM 0.00, depending on the specific device and the user's profile.
2. Monthly Subscription Amount: A fixed monthly fee that you pay throughout the duration of your subscription.

Key Features:
- No Complex Tenure Matrix: There are no confusing tables to navigate. Each device variant has a clear, fixed price.
- Pricing per Variant: Pricing is determined at the product variant level. This means you can choose your preferred storage capacity or specific model and see the exact deposit and monthly amount immediately.

Data Structure:
For developers and system integrators, each model's pricing is represented in a clean JSON structure within its respective file:
{
  "model": "...",
  "brand": "...",
  "variants": [
    {
      "id": N,
      "title": "...",
      "deposit": {
        "value": N,
        "formatted": "RM ..."
      },
      "amount": {
        "value": N,
        "formatted": "RM ..."
      },
      "image": {
        "original": "...",
        "medium": "..."
      }
    }
  ]
}

This structure allows for multiple variants (e.g., 128GB vs 256GB) to be listed under a single model, each with its own specific pricing details.
