GoFlexi Application Process

The GoFlexi application process is streamlined to be as quick and effortless as possible, moving away from traditional, paperwork-heavy methods to a modern, personalized experience powered by e-KYC (electronic Know Your Customer) verification.

Step 1: Personal Data
The applicant provides their basic personal information — full name as per IC, date of birth, IC number, and other required personal details. This forms the foundation of the e-KYC verification process.

Step 2: Identity Verification
The applicant undergoes identity verification, typically by uploading a photo of their MyKad (IC) and a live selfie for facial matching. This ensures the person applying is who they claim to be — a standard e-KYC security measure.

Step 3: Residency Address
The applicant provides their current residential address. This may be used for device delivery and verification purposes.

Step 4: Emergency Contact
The applicant provides an emergency contact person — name, relationship, and phone number. This is a safety measure in case we need to reach someone on the applicant's behalf.

Step 5: Occupation
The applicant provides their employment details — occupation type, employer name, and monthly income range. This step is important because it determines which supporting documents are required (e.g., payslip, employer letter, bank statements) to proceed with the application.

Step 6: Phone Model
The applicant selects their preferred phone model from the available GoFlexi catalog. They can browse by brand (Apple, Samsung, HONOR, OPPO, Google) and choose the specific model and storage variant they want.

Step 7: Email Verification
The applicant verifies their email address by clicking a confirmation link sent to their inbox. This ensures we have a valid communication channel for application updates and offer notifications.

Step 8: Application Review & Phone Offer
Once all steps are completed and submitted, the GoAnsuran team reviews the application. Instead of a one-size-fits-all approach, we create a personalized phone offer based on the applicant's profile and selected device. A team member will reach out to present this tailored offer, including the deposit amount and monthly subscription fee. The applicant then reviews and accepts the offer to proceed with device delivery.

Key Highlights:
- e-KYC Powered: Fast, digital identity verification — no physical document submission needed.
- No Complex Paperwork: We've eliminated the traditional bureaucracy of gadget financing.
- Personalized Offers: Every offer is reviewed by a human team to ensure it fits the applicant's profile.
- Quick Turnaround: Designed for speed so you can get your device faster.
