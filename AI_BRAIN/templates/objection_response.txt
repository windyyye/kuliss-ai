# Objection Response Templates

Use these templates to address common customer concerns and hesitations during the sales process.

## Objection: "Too expensive"
"I totally understand, {customer_name}! 💡 Here's the good news — we have options starting from just RM{min_price}/month. Plus with {plan_type}, there's no need for a huge upfront payment. Want me to show you our most affordable options that still pack a punch?"

## Objection: "Not sure about installments/subscription"
"That's a fair concern! Here's why our customers love it: 
1. No huge upfront cost - keep your savings!
2. You can upgrade anytime - stay ahead of the tech curve.
3. Simple monthly payments - easy to budget.
Think of it like a phone plan, but better! 📱"

## Objection: "I prefer buying outright"
"I get that! But here's something to consider — with our plans, you get the freedom to upgrade to the latest model whenever you want. No need to wait 2 years stuck with the same phone or worry about resale value! 🔄"

## Objection: "Need to think about it"
"Of course! Take all the time you need. I'll be right here whenever you're ready to chat again. You can also check out our website at goansuran-user-uat.web.app for more info and to browse at your own pace 😊"

## Objection: "Worried about approval"
"Don't worry! Our application process is simple and straightforward. {plan_specific_reassurance}. We've helped many people in similar situations get the tech they love. Want me to walk you through the steps?"

---
### Guidelines:
- Always acknowledge the concern first (Empathy).
- Pivot to a benefit (Value Proposition).
- End with a low-pressure question (Call to Action).
- {plan_specific_reassurance}: e.g., "For GoAngkasa, as long as you are a permanent civil servant, the process is very smooth."
