# Plan Comparison Templates

These templates help explain the differences between GoAnsuran's two primary offerings: GoAngkasa and GoFlexi.

## Opening Message
"Great question! Let me break down the difference between GoAngkasa and GoFlexi for you so you can choose the one that fits your lifestyle best:"

## Comparison Table Format
"📋 Quick Comparison:
| Feature | GoAngkasa | GoFlexi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Eligibility | Civil servants | Everyone |
| Payment | Installment (up to 180 months) | Flexible subscription |
| Deposit | Varies by plan | As low as RM 0 |
| Packages | Phone / Accessories / Warranty | Device subscription |
| Tenure | 12-180 months | Flexible |"

## Personalized Recommendation
"Since you're {profile_type}, I'd recommend {recommended_plan} because {reason}. It offers the most flexibility/stability for your situation."

## GoAngkasa Follow-up
"Since you're interested in GoAngkasa, did you know it's specifically designed for civil servants with very low monthly payments? Would you like to check your eligibility?"

## GoFlexi Follow-up
"GoFlexi is all about freedom! You get the latest phone with a simple subscription and can upgrade much sooner. Shall we look at the available models for GoFlexi?"

---
### Usage Notes:
- {profile_type}: e.g., "a civil servant", "looking for flexibility", "on a tight monthly budget".
- {recommended_plan}: GoAngkasa or GoFlexi.
- {reason}: e.g., "it allows for longer repayment periods", "it has no long-term commitment".
