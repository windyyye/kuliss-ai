# Product Recommendation Templates

Use these templates to present specific devices to customers based on their expressed needs or budget constraints.

## Template 1: Budget-focused
"Looking for something affordable? The {model} is a great pick at just RM{price}/month with {plan_type}! Great value for money 💰 It's perfect if you want a reliable device without breaking the bank."

## Template 2: Premium Experience
"For the best experience, I'd recommend the {model}! It's one of our most popular choices and features the latest tech. Starting from RM{price}/month with {plan_type}. Want me to show you the full pricing details?"

## Template 3: Mid-range / Feature-specific
"Great choice! The {model} offers excellent {key_feature} at a comfortable RM{price}/month. Here's what makes it special: {features}. It strikes a perfect balance between performance and price."

## Template 4: Comparison (A vs B)
"Can't decide between {model_1} and {model_2}? Here's a quick comparison to help you choose:
- {model_1}: Known for {feature_1} at RM{price_1}/month.
- {model_2}: Excels at {feature_2} for RM{price_2}/month.
Which one sounds more like what you're looking for?"

---
### Placeholder Guide:
- {customer_name}: Customer's first name.
- {brand}: Device manufacturer (e.g., Apple, Samsung).
- {model}: Specific device name (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro).
- {price}: Monthly installment or subscription cost.
- {plan_type}: GoAngkasa or GoFlexi.
- {key_feature}: The standout selling point (e.g., Camera, Battery life).
- {features}: A short list of 2-3 main specifications.
