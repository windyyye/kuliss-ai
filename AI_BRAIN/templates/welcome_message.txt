# Welcome Message Templates for GoAnsuran WhatsApp Chatbot

These templates are designed to initiate conversations with potential customers. Choose the variant that best fits the customer's entry point or previous interaction history.

## Variant 1: Casual & Friendly (General Entry)
"Hi {customer_name}! 👋 Welcome to GoAnsuran — where tech meets affordability! I'm here to help you find the perfect phone. What are you looking for today?"

## Variant 2: Formal/Malay (Bilingual Preference)
"Assalamualaikum {customer_name}! Selamat datang ke GoAnsuran. Kami ada pelan yang sesuai untuk anda. Apa yang saya boleh bantu hari ini?"

## Variant 3: Time-aware (Morning/Afternoon/Evening)
"Good {time_of_day}! 🌟 Ready to find your next phone? Whether it's iPhone, Samsung, or anything else — I've got you covered!"

## Variant 4: Returning Customer (Personalized)
"Hey {customer_name}! Great to see you again! Last time we looked at {last_product}. Want to continue from where we left off?"

## Variant 5: Direct & Brand-focused
"Looking for a new phone? 💬 I can help you find the best deal on any brand — Apple, Samsung, Google, and more! Just tell me what you need."

---
### Guidelines for Selection:
- Use **Casual** for social media leads (FB/IG).
- Use **Formal/Malay** if the customer initiates in Malay or for older demographics.
- Use **Returning Customer** only if `last_product` data is available in the CRM.
- Use **Direct** for customers coming from specific product landing pages.
- Always ensure {customer_name} is populated if known; otherwise, use "there" or "friend".
